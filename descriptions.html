Image descriptions

This page provides a plain-text version of the visual descriptions and captions for The Staph Aureus Experience™, a digital gallery of Polaroid-style collages by Alice van Rooyen / Apple Violet Robot.

The gallery itself presents the images in a shuffled order. This page lists the descriptions in the order used by the project data file.

    Caption reads: Alien brains coming out of my hand. No one warned me not to look!
